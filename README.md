## Hey there :wave:

I’m Ere, a web artisan from Helsinki. I build websites that look and feel great. Being creative, solving problems and getting the small details right is what I do.

## What I do

At [NoA Ignite](https://noaignite.com), I help develop and maintain the Duet design system at LähiTapiola and build accessible HTML prototypes. I also work on Reima’s product information service.

I make small open-source packages for [Astro](https://astro.build):

- [astro-i18n](https://github.com/eremannisto/astro-i18n) adds locale routing, language detection and translations.
- [astro-masonry](https://github.com/eremannisto/astro-masonry) renders a responsive masonry layout with keyboard navigation.
- [astro-metadata](https://github.com/eremannisto/astro-metadata) manages page metadata, social previews and favicons.

And a few tools for the browser, such as a [password generator](https://password.mannisto.io).
